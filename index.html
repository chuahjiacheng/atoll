Atoll

A small "island" overlay for Windows 10 and 11. It sits at the top centre of your screen and shows what's happening at a glance, then expands into a panel when you need more.

What it does

  • Claude Code: live activity from your coding sessions, with permission prompts you can answer in place.
  • Calendar: your upcoming Google Calendar events and a month view. Atoll only reads your calendar and never changes it.
  • Music: what's playing in Spotify, with synced lyrics.
  • Everyday tools: clipboard history, a whiteboard, a file tray, a quick note and a focus timer.

Your data stays on your PC

Atoll is a desktop app. It has no accounts, no servers of its own and no telemetry. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses and why.
