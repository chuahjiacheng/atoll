Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Atoll is a desktop app for Windows. It runs on your computer and has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives your data.

Google Calendar

If you choose Connect Google Calendar, Atoll asks Google for read-only access to your calendars (the calendar.readonly permission). It uses that access to:

  • list your calendars, so you can pick which ones appear in the island;
  • read events from about one month before today to two months after, so it can show what's coming up and a month view.

Atoll never creates, changes or deletes anything in your calendar.

Where it goes: calendar data is fetched directly from Google to your PC about every 5 minutes, kept in memory to display it, and not saved to disk or sent anywhere else. Your Google sign-in (refresh token) is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. The short-lived access token is kept only in memory.

Sharing: Atoll does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and no person reads it.

Disconnecting: choose Disconnect in Atoll's Settings → Calendar. Atoll revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored sign-in from your PC. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Atoll's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other features

  • Spotify: if you connect Spotify, Atoll reads what's playing from Spotify to show it in the island. Song lyrics are looked up on LRCLIB by track name and artist.
  • Claude Code: Atoll reads activity from Claude Code sessions on your PC and, to show your usage, asks Anthropic for your usage figures using your existing Claude sign-in.
  • Clipboard, notes, whiteboard and files: stored only on your PC.

Children

Atoll is not directed at children under 13.
